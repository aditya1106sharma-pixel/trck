# TRCK for friends

TRCK is a free logbook for a 60 day body recomposition: eat enough protein, lift three or four times a week, and cut back on cigarettes and tea or coffee. You log food, sets and habits each day, and it gives you a score out of 100. It is made by Aditya and has no ads.

The numbers are rough guides. They are not medical advice.

## Open it
Go to **https://aditya1106sharma-pixel.github.io/trck/** on your phone. The first time, it asks for your body weight and a start date, then sets a protein target. You can change every number later in the Plan tab.

## Put it on your home screen
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu (three dots), then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

After that it opens like an app and keeps working with no signal, which is handy in a gym basement.

## Where your log lives
Your log is saved on your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you make an account. Clearing your browser data, or losing the phone, deletes the log, so back it up.

## Back it up
Plan, then **Download backup**. Do it once a week. To bring a log back, or move to a new phone, open Plan, then **Restore or import**, and pick the file. Before an import, TRCK keeps a copy of what was on the phone.

## Use it on two devices (optional)
Plan, then Sync, then **Create account** with an email and a password you will remember. Sign in the same way on your other device and your log follows you.

Signing out clears the log from that device, so the next person to use it starts fresh. Your account keeps your log, and a copy stays on the device under Plan, Your data, Copies kept on this device.

Two things to know before you do:
- **There is no password reset yet.** If you forget the password, the cloud copy is out of reach. Your backup file still works.
- **Aditya can technically see saved logs.** They are stored in his free Supabase project, and the owner of a project can open its tables. Do not put anything in your notes that you would not want him to read.

## Updates
When a new version is ready, a bar says **Update ready**. Tap it. The version number is at the bottom of Plan.

## Something wrong or annoying?
Tell Aditya what you were doing and what you expected. Small things matter most: they get fixed first.
